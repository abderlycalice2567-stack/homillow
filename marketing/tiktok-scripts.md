# 🎬 Hearth — 20 Ready-to-Film TikTok Scripts

Each one: a **Hook** (say it AND put it on screen in the first second), what to **Film**,
the **Caption**, and **audio** guidance. Most are 15–30 seconds. Film on your phone, vertical.

> 💡 Reuse the winners. When one hits, make 3 more like it. TikTok rewards variations of what works.

---

## PILLAR 1 — RELATABLE PAIN / SKITS (post the most of these)

### 1. The Default Parent
- **Hook (on screen + say it):** "POV: you're the parent who remembers *everything.*"
- **Film:** You/wife looking exhausted, then rattle off: "dentist at 10, soccer at 5, permission slip due, we're out of milk, and it's your mom's birthday." End staring into camera, dead inside.
- **Caption:** The mental load is a full-time job nobody applied for. There's finally an app for it. #mentalload #defaultparent #momtok
- **Audio:** trending "tired mom" or comedic overwhelmed sound.

### 2. The Text Fight
- **Hook:** "Every couple has had this exact fight 👇"
- **Film:** Screen-recording of texts: "Did you pick up Sarah?" "I thought YOU had her??" "..." Then cut to Hearth showing "Sarah pickup — assigned: Dad — 3:15pm."
- **Caption:** No more "I thought you had it." It's just… on the screen now. #marriedlife #parentinghacks #hearthapp
- **Audio:** tense-to-calm trending sound.

### 3. What's For Dinner
- **Hook:** "If I hear 'what's for dinner' one more time—"
- **Film:** You mid-chaos, kids yelling, then calmly turn phone around showing Hearth meal plan + grocery list.
- **Caption:** The answer's already in the app. Peace. #momlife #mealplanning #momhacks

### 4. The Invisible Load (talking to camera)
- **Hook:** "Nobody sees the 47 things running in my head right now."
- **Film:** You, close-up, calm voice, listing the invisible tasks. End: "So I put all of it in one place my whole family can see."
- **Caption:** If you're the one holding it all — this one's for you. 🤍 #mentalload #overwhelmedmom

### 5. Sunday Scaries, Parent Edition
- **Hook:** "Sunday night as a parent = trying to remember the entire week from memory."
- **Film:** You panicking with a paper calendar, sticky notes everywhere → cut to calm Hearth weekly view.
- **Caption:** I don't do the Sunday panic anymore. #momsoftiktok #familyorganization

## 🎥 FILMING CHEAT SHEET
- **Vertical**, phone at eye level, good light (face a window).
- **First second = hook** on screen + spoken. No slow intros.
- **Add captions** (TikTok auto-captions, then edit typos).
- **Keep it under 30s** to start; you can go longer once people watch.
- **End with a soft CTA:** "It's called Hearth" / "Link in bio."
- **Batch film:** shoot 5–10 in one sitting wearing the same outfit, post over the week.
