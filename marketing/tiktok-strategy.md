# 🔥 Hearth — TikTok Marketing Strategy

*The playbook. Read this once, then live in the scripts file.*

---

## 🎯 THE ONE-LINE STRATEGY
Sell the **feeling** (a calm, organized home), not the app. On TikTok, nobody wants
"a family calendar." They want to stop feeling like they're drowning. Show the drowning,
then show the calm. Hearth is the calm.

## 👥 WHO WE'RE TALKING TO (in order)
1. **Moms 28–45 ("the default parent")** — carries the whole household in her head. This is
   90% of your audience. "MomTok" is one of the biggest communities on the platform.
2. **Overwhelmed couples** — the "who's picking up the kids?" fight.
3. **Dads who want to step up** — help me carry my share.

Speak to the MOM first. She downloads it, she adds the husband, she tells 5 friends.

## 🧱 THE 5 CONTENT PILLARS (rotate these)
1. **Relatable pain / skits** 😩 — "POV: you're the one who remembers everything." Biggest reach.
2. **Validate the mental load** 🧠 — name the invisible work. Moms feel *seen*, they share it.
3. **Product demo** 📱 — screen-record Hearth solving the exact problem from the hook.
4. **Build-in-public / founder** 👨‍👧 — a dad + pastor building this for his own family. Authentic gold.
5. **Quick organization tips** 💡 — pure value; some mention Hearth, some don't (builds trust).

Rough mix per week: **40% pain/relatable, 20% demo, 20% founder, 20% tips.**

## 📅 POSTING CADENCE
- **1–3 posts/day.** Volume wins on TikTok — most accounts blow up on video #30–80, not #3.
- Best windows (Eastern): ~**6–8am**, **12pm**, **7–10pm** (parents scrolling in bed).
- **Consistency > perfection.** Ugly authentic phone video beats polished ad every time.

## 🪝 THE RULES THAT ACTUALLY MATTER
- **Hook in the first 1 second.** Text on screen + say it out loud. If the first line is boring, it's dead.
- **Native, not an ad.** Film it like a mom talking to a friend, not a commercial.
- **Trending audio** on every post (even low volume under your talking) — TikTok pushes videos using trending sounds.
- **On-screen captions always** — most watch on mute.
- **One CTA, soft:** "Link in bio" or "It's called Hearth." Don't hard-sell.
- **Reply to every comment with a video** — the cheapest way to go viral is a video reply to "what app is this?"

## 📈 GROWTH TACTICS (beyond posting)
- **Series/hooks that repeat** — "Mental load diaries" ep. 1, 2, 3… people follow for the next one.
- **Duet/stitch** big momtok creators' "I'm so overwhelmed" videos with "here's what fixed it for me."
- **Comment-bait:** end with a question ("what's the one thing your family always forgets?").
- **Whitelist your best organic video into a $5–10/day TikTok ad** *only after* one hits — amplify winners, don't boost losers.
- **Get 10 real families using it → screen-record their reactions → UGC gold.**

## 🎨 BRAND KIT (keep it consistent)
- **Colors:** ember `#c2582f`, warm amber `#e08a4a`, deep calm navy `#20303f`, cream `#f4f1ec`
- **Vibe:** warm, calm, honest, a little funny about the chaos. Never corporate.
- **Font feel:** clean, rounded, friendly.
- **Logo:** the home-with-a-heart icon.

## 🧭 THE 30-DAY LAUNCH ARC
- **Week 1:** Founder story + "why I built this." Introduce yourself. Low sell.
- **Week 2:** Hammer the pain (relatable skits) + first demos.
- **Week 3:** Value tips + demos + reply-videos to comments.
- **Week 4:** Testimonials/UGC + a "we just launched" push to link in bio.
