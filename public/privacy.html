Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026
The short version: Homillow holds your family's information so your household can run in one calm place. We never sell your data, we never serve ads, and we never share your family's information except to provide the service. Parents are always in control of their family's — and their children's — data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Homillow ("Homillow," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Homillow application and website at homillow.com (the "Service"). If you have questions, contact us at homillow.family@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a password (which we store only as a securely hashed value — we never see or store your actual password).
  • Family content: the information you and your family members add to the Service — calendar events, tasks and chores, grocery items, goals, saved moments, prayer entries, display names, member roles, colors, and birthdays.
  • Children's information: any information a parent or guardian chooses to add about a child (such as a name, birthday, schedule, or chores). See Section 6.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and basic log data needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Local storage: we store your login session on your own device (in your browser's local storage) so you stay signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Payment information

Subscription payments are processed by our payment provider, Stripe. Homillow does not receive or store your full card number. Stripe handles card data under its own privacy policy and security standards.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and sync the Service across your family's devices.
  • To create and secure your account and keep each family's data isolated from every other family.
  • To process subscriptions and send you service-related messages (for example, billing or security notices).
  • To respond to your requests and provide support.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or security threats.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, share it with advertisers, or use your family's content to build advertising or marketing profiles.

4. How your information is shared

We share information only in these limited ways:

  • Within your family: content you add is visible to the members of your family, according to the roles and permissions your family sets.
  • Service providers: trusted vendors who help us run the Service — our hosting provider (Render), our payment processor (Stripe), and a weather data provider (Open-Meteo) for briefing forecasts. They may process data only to perform services for us.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Homillow, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfer: if Homillow is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred, and we will notify you of any change in ownership or use of your personal information.

5. Data security

We take the protection of family data seriously. Passwords are hashed with industry-standard bcrypt hashing. Connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Each family's data is logically isolated so that one family cannot access another family's information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to promptly address any issues we discover.

6. Children's privacy

Homillow is designed for parents and guardians to manage their household. Only an adult (18 or older) may create an account and a family. Children do not create their own accounts and do not independently sign up for or interact with the Service.

A parent or guardian may choose to add information about their own children (such as a name, birthday, or schedule) to their family. When they do:

  • That information is used solely to provide the Service to that family.
  • We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never disclose it outside the family except as described in Section 4.
  • The parent or guardian controls that information and can view, edit, or delete it at any time from within the app, or by contacting us.

Because children's information is provided and controlled by a parent or guardian, Homillow relies on that adult's consent for its collection and use. If you believe a child's information has been provided to us without proper authority, contact us at homillow.family@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, or ask us to, we delete your family's content, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most of your information directly in the app.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and your family's data by contacting homillow.family@gmail.com.
  • Export: you can request a copy of your family's data.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA (for example, the right to object to or restrict certain processing). We honor these rights where they apply. We do not sell personal information.

9. International users

Homillow is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be processed and stored in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app or by email. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email us at homillow.family@gmail.com.

Homillow does not serve ads and does not sell your personal information.
